Module bank 

Source
Expand description

The banking attribute macros, namespaced as bank::*.

bank::module!() declares a bank group, #[bank::main] the bank-0 entry point, and #[bank::zero] a bank-0 helper. (The bare #[bank] attribute is a sibling in the macro namespace, not in this module.)

Macros§

Declare a submodule as part of its parent module’s bank group.
module
Declare the enclosing module as a ROM bank group.
